Prop

Cigarette case

Date October 2023
Budget €5
Team Individual

This prop was part of a university project where we had to recreate a cigarette case from the 50s to appear on a show about a man that had some dark business and a tobacco family business. The case had to appear “used” and be a very important hallmark in the movie.

First I did some research and a moodboard of what I wanted and needed, then I took an old sewing kit case and painted it, designed the stickers and put everything together. Then I made the cigarrettes and made everything look used by sanding and messing up the paint.
