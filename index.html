About Me

Hello, this is Nieves, I am a 23 years old girl from Spain. I love nature, horses, painting and baking in my free time. I grew up in a small town in Alicante called Aspe, and when I was 15 I went to study abroad to Virginia where I fell in love with the U.S.A. After studying media in Gandia, Spain for a few years I decided to do an exchange year in Italy at a fine arts accademy focusing in Scenography.

I’ve always dreamed of being a film director but as I grew I realized that I loved art directing, and working with my hands. My pasion in film is to make props and working on finding the atrezzo for different ideas. Now I’m back to Spain and hoping to start studying Art directing as a masters degree soon.
