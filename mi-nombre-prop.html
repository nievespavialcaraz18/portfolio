Prop

Lolita's diary

Date January 2024
Budget €9
Team Individual

For my university short film “Mi nombre” i was in charge of props since i love doing arts and crafts and working with my hands. This time I had to make 4 diaries for when Lolita was a little girl and one for when she is grown to show the evolution of the girl's emotions through her drawings and writing, as well as the passage of time reflected in details such as the dirt, the missing parts, etc.

I took 4 notebooks that looked the same, turned them into the four versions of the same one I needed and wrote and draw on every page just in case. Then for the new one I changed the design to show the maturity of the girl.
