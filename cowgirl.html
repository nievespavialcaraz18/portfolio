Photography

Cowgirl

Date February 2026
Budget €0
Team Individual

The photoshoot is inspired by the kind done in the United States (for example, in Virginia) when a country girl graduates or just likes music and horses. It’s an aesthetic I admire because it reminds me of country singers and life on the ranch, it feels like a movie but I have seen people that just live like that and its their reality.

With this, I want to connect with my cowgirl side, a part of me that has always made me very happy, and a way of life that feels so positive to me, because I grew up around horses and my host family in the U.S. have always showed me this type of life that now I miss and cherish.
