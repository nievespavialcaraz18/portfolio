Scenography

Mi nombre

Date January 2024
Budget €1000
Team 30 people

This set was designed to be the bedroom of Lolita since “Mi nombre” was a university project of a shortfilm inspired in Vladimir Nabokov's novel (with a feminist point of view).

My classmates and I worked together and in groups such as the art department, where I was in charge of atrezzo and props as one of the assistants, we raised money, bought some cheap materials and gathered things from our families homes. Then we created this bedroom with only XPS foam and wall paper.
